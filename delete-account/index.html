Delete your account and data

You can delete your Habit Streaks account and its cloud data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Habit Streaks and tap the settings icon.
  2. Under "Cloud backup & sync", tap Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

This takes effect straight away and needs an internet connection.

By email, without the app

Email abhijeetkumar.workonly@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Habit Streaks account", from the Google account you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we will complete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Habit Streaks sign-in account, including your email address and account identifier
  • All your habits, check-ins, notes, schedules and reminder times stored in the cloud

What is not deleted

  • Data on your phone: habits on your device stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Your Android backup: this belongs to your Google account and is managed in your Android or Google settings.
  • Purchases: Google Play keeps its own purchase records. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so please cancel it in Google Play.
  • Advertising data: this is handled by Google under its own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

We keep no copy of your cloud habit data after deletion. Read the full privacy policy.
