Habit Streaks by Olfoxacin

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Habit Streaks is a habit tracking app for Android, published on Google Play by Olfoxacin. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account. Your habits then stay on your phone and in your own Android backup.
  • If you sign in with Google, your habits are stored in the cloud so they can sync and survive a new phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Pro removes them.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your phone

Your habits, check-ins, notes, reminder times and settings are stored on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android can include them in your personal Google backup. You can export or import your history as a CSV file at any time.

Data stored if you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, we use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, both provided by Google, to store:

  • Account details: your email address and Google account identifier, used to sign you in and keep your data under your account.
  • Your habit data: habit names, icons, colours, schedules, goals, reminder times, check-ins and any notes you write, used to back up and sync your habits.

Only your signed-in account can read or change this data through the app. It is encrypted in transit and stored on Google's servers in Mumbai, India. Please avoid putting sensitive information in habit names or notes.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner on the habit detail screen and offers an optional rewarded ad. Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change your choice in Settings under "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Pro purchases are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details. The app only checks which Pro purchases your Google account owns.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and the daily check-in from Ember, the app's mascot, are created on your phone from your own habit data. You can turn them off in your Android settings.

Sharing

We share data only with the Google services named above, which process it to run the app, or where the law requires it. We do not sell your data or share your habit data with advertisers.

Keeping and deleting your data

Cloud data is kept until you delete it. In the app, go to Settings, then "Delete account and cloud data", or follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting a habit also removes its history from the cloud. Uninstalling the app removes data from your phone, but it does not delete your cloud account.

Children

Habit Streaks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
