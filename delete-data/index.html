Delete some of your data

You can delete a habit, or a note, without deleting your Habit Streaks account.

Delete a habit and its history

  1. Open Habit Streaks and tap the habit.
  2. Tap the menu icon at the top right, then tap Delete.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This removes the habit and its check-ins and notes from your phone. If you are signed in, it also removes them from the cloud the next time your phone is online.

Delete a note

  1. Open the habit and press and hold the day in the calendar.
  2. Clear the note and tap Save.

By email, without the app

Email abhijeetkumar.workonly@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, and tell us which habits or notes to delete. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we will complete it within 30 days.

What is kept

After you delete a habit, a small record with its name, settings and deletion marker stays so other devices know to remove it. Your account, your email address and your other habits are kept until you delete your account. To remove everything, see delete your account and data.
